Before you submit

Check the add-on before you submit it.

A review skill for one add-on folder. It reads the source against Adobe’s current docs, walks the panel at 320px in light and dark, and prepares the listing. It does not open Adobe Express, and it does not submit anything.

What it does

Scan the source

Checks are tied to a sentence in Adobe’s current docs. When that sentence is gone, the check is skipped. A few more come from real marketplace rejection letters. Comments are ignored.

Walk the panel

Headless Chrome opens the panel at 320px, in light and dark, and presses the screens it can reach. A close button at the bottom of a dialog, a covered control, clipped text, and a dialog with no way out are fixed and measured again.

Prepare the listing

Name, summary, description, and keywords are drafted from the manifest and the panel. Screenshots use one locked 1360×800 template and only words that are already in the add-on. You approve them before anything is written.

How a review goes

  1. 01

    The agent scans the add-on and writes a report outside the project, in ~/.express-addon-review/.

  2. 02

    You choose once where to follow along: a local dashboard in Chrome, or the chat.

  3. 03

    It walks the panel. Measured bugs are fixed without a question. Warnings wait for one “fix everything” choice.

  4. 04

    The remaining choices are a test license when a screen is locked, an optional polish pass, design, and the listing. Never one question per finding.

  5. 05

    It keeps going until the review is finished. If a pasted rejection letter started the review, it ends with notes for the resubmission.

Install

The download is this project. It does not require access to the repository. Node.js 18 or newer is required. The panel walk and the listing screenshots need Chrome, Edge, or Chromium on the same computer. pdftotext is used for the brand PDF when it is installed.

Then open the add-on in an agent that loads the skill and ask it to review that folder. The same installer links the skill for Claude, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, Windsurf, and the other agents the installer already knows.

What it leaves to you

  • Other browsers, and one pass on a slow network. Reviewers try Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox.
  • The publisher website, the publisher form, and test credentials when the add-on logs in or has a paid feature.
  • Screens behind a login the walk cannot reach, and whether the wording actually makes sense.

A finished review is not marketplace approval. Adobe’s reviewers never see the local dashboard. The dashboard stays on your computer.
